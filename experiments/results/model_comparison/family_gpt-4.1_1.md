# Kyoto Family Garden & Food Adventure

## Trip summary
Enjoy four leisurely days in Kyoto, exploring its serene gardens and sampling delicious local cuisine, tailored for a family with young children (ages 5 and 8). The itinerary prioritizes a relaxed pace, includes a midday rest each day, and avoids nightlife, focusing instead on family-friendly experiences and memorable meals.

## Assumptions
- Travel dates are unspecified; planning assumes typical spring or autumn weather.
- Accommodation is a mid-range family-friendly hotel or ryokan.
- All costs are per person (children may pay less for some entries/meals).
- Flights are excluded.
- No dietary restrictions specified.
- Local transport is primarily by bus, subway, or short taxi rides.

## Day-by-day itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival & Southern Higashiyama
- **Morning:** Arrive in Kyoto. Settle into your accommodation and enjoy a gentle stroll in Maruyama Park, famous for its cherry trees and open spaces for kids to play.
- **Afternoon:** Visit Kodai-ji Temple and its tranquil gardens. Lunch at a nearby family-friendly café offering Japanese set meals.
- **Evening:** Early dinner at a local izakaya with private rooms (many welcome children), then return to the hotel for rest.

### Day 2: Arashiyama & Bamboo Grove
- **Morning:** Head to Arashiyama. Explore the Bamboo Grove and visit Tenryu-ji Temple’s beautiful gardens.
- **Afternoon:** Lunch at a riverside restaurant with kid-friendly options. Take a short boat ride or visit the Iwatayama Monkey Park if the children are energetic. Return to accommodation for a midday rest.
- **Evening:** Casual dinner near your hotel, such as ramen or udon, before relaxing for the night.

### Day 3: Northern Kyoto & Imperial Gardens
- **Morning:** Visit the Kyoto Imperial Palace Park, where children can run in open spaces and adults can admire the landscaping.
- **Afternoon:** Lunch at a nearby café, then stroll through the Kyoto Botanical Gardens, which feature themed areas and a conservatory. Return to your accommodation for a rest.
- **Evening:** Try a kaiseki (multi-course) meal at a family-friendly restaurant, or opt for a simple donburi (rice bowl) spot.

### Day 4: Fushimi Inari & Departure
- **Morning:** Visit Fushimi Inari Taisha early to avoid crowds. Walk partway up the torii gate path (as far as the children are comfortable).
- **Afternoon:** Lunch at a local eatery specializing in Kyoto-style sushi or inari-zushi (sweet tofu pockets). Return to your accommodation to pack and rest before departure.
- **Evening:** Depart Kyoto or enjoy a final stroll if time allows.

## Practical travel tips
- Kyoto’s public transport is efficient and stroller-friendly, but some temple areas have steps.
- Many gardens and temples offer discounted or free entry for young children.
- Consider renting a pocket Wi-Fi for easy navigation.
- Spring and autumn are mild; bring layers and rain gear just in case.
- Most restaurants provide high chairs and children’s menus, but it’s good to check in advance.
- For midday rests, choose accommodation with comfortable communal areas or family rooms.
- Currency: Japanese Yen (JPY). Many places accept credit cards, but some smaller shops and temples are cash-only.
