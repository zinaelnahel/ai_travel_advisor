# Accessible Berlin: A Cultural Adventure

## Trip summary
This itinerary covers 2 days in Berlin, focusing on museums and vegetarian dining options, tailored for a wheelchair user. The plan emphasizes accessible venues and activities, ensuring a comfortable and enriching experience.

## Assumptions
- Departure city is unknown.
- Budget is EUR 400 excluding flights.
- All venues and transport options are assumed to be wheelchair accessible, but it's recommended to verify accessibility details in advance.

## Day-by-day itinerary
### Day 1: Museum Island Exploration
- **Morning:** Start your day at the **Pergamon Museum**, known for its impressive ancient artifacts. The museum is wheelchair accessible, with ramps and elevators available.
- **Afternoon:** Enjoy lunch at **Kreuzberger Himmel**, a vegetarian restaurant offering a range of delicious dishes. Afterward, visit the **Neues Museum**, which is also accessible and houses a vast collection of Egyptian artifacts and more.
- **Evening:** Dine at **Lokal**, a cozy vegetarian restaurant that focuses on seasonal ingredients. The restaurant is accessible and offers a warm atmosphere.

### Day 2: Contemporary Art and Culture
- **Morning:** Visit the **Berlinische Galerie**, which showcases modern art and photography. The gallery is fully accessible with elevators and ramps.
- **Afternoon:** Have lunch at **The Bowl**, a popular vegetarian spot known for its healthy bowls and smoothies. After lunch, head to the **Jewish Museum Berlin**, which is wheelchair accessible and provides an insightful look into Jewish history and culture.
- **Evening:** Conclude your trip with dinner at **Chada Thai**, a vegetarian-friendly Thai restaurant that offers a variety of flavorful dishes. Ensure to check for accessibility details when making a reservation.

## Practical travel tips
- Berlin's public transport system is generally wheelchair accessible, with many buses and trains equipped with ramps.
- Consider using the **Berlin Welcome Card**, which offers unlimited travel on public transport and discounts on various attractions.
- Always verify the accessibility of specific venues before your visit, as conditions may change.
- Weather in Berlin can be variable; check the forecast closer to your travel dates for appropriate clothing.
