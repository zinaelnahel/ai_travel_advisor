# Accessible Berlin Museums & Vegetarian Delights

## Trip summary
This 2-day trip in Berlin is tailored for a wheelchair user interested in exploring museums and enjoying vegetarian cuisine. The itinerary focuses on accessible venues with minimal stairs, highlighting key museums and vegetarian-friendly restaurants. The budget is EUR 400 per person, excluding flights.

## Assumptions
- Arrival and departure in Berlin city center.
- Mobility needs require wheelchair accessibility; stairs avoided or flagged for verification.
- Budget excludes flights.
- No specific dietary restrictions beyond vegetarian.
- Travel dates not specified; itinerary assumes moderate weather.

## Day-by-day itinerary

### Day 1: Museum Island & Vegetarian Dining
- **Morning:** Visit the Pergamon Museum on Museum Island. This museum is wheelchair accessible with elevators and ramps; verify current accessibility details before visiting.
- **Afternoon:** Explore the Neues Museum, also on Museum Island, which houses Egyptian artifacts and is wheelchair accessible with elevators.
- **Evening:** Dinner at "Kopps," a well-known vegetarian restaurant with wheelchair access and a cozy atmosphere.

### Day 2: Modern Art & Cultural Exploration
- **Morning:** Visit the Hamburger Bahnhof – Museum für Gegenwart (Museum of Contemporary Art). It is wheelchair accessible with elevators.
- **Afternoon:** Explore the Jewish Museum Berlin, which offers excellent accessibility features including ramps and elevators.
- **Evening:** Dinner at "The Bowl," a fully vegan and wheelchair-accessible restaurant focusing on healthy bowls and smoothies.

## Practical travel tips
- Berlin’s public transport (U-Bahn, S-Bahn, trams, and buses) is largely wheelchair accessible; check specific stations for elevator availability.
- Purchase a Berlin WelcomeCard for unlimited public transport and discounts at many museums.
- Confirm accessibility details with museums and restaurants ahead of visit as renovations or temporary closures may affect access.
- Weather in Berlin can vary; check forecast closer to travel dates for appropriate clothing.
- Currency: Euro (EUR). Use local ATMs or exchange services; credit cards widely accepted.
