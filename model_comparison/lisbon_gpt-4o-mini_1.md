# Culinary and Architectural Delights in Lisbon

## Trip summary
Explore the vibrant city of Lisbon over three days, focusing on its rich culinary scene and stunning architecture. From traditional Portuguese dishes to iconic landmarks, this trip will immerse you in the flavors and sights of this beautiful city.

- Moderate budget.

- Travel dates are not specified.

## Day-by-day itinerary
### Day 1: Historic Center and Traditional Cuisine
- **Morning:** Arrive in Lisbon and check into your accommodation. Start your day with a visit to the Praça do Comércio, a stunning square by the river. Explore the nearby Rua Augusta and its impressive arch.
- **Afternoon:** Head to the Alfama district, known for its narrow streets and historic buildings. Enjoy lunch at a local tasca (small restaurant) serving traditional dishes like bacalhau à brás (codfish). Visit the Lisbon Cathedral and the Miradouro de Santa Luzia for beautiful views.
- **Evening:** Dine at a restaurant in Alfama, perhaps trying some grilled sardines. Consider catching a Fado performance for an authentic cultural experience.

### Day 2: Belém and Modern Architecture
- **Morning:** Take a tram or bus to Belém. Visit the iconic Belém Tower and the Jerónimos Monastery, both UNESCO World Heritage sites. Don't forget to stop by Pastéis de Belém for their famous custard tarts.
- **Afternoon:** Explore the MAAT (Museum of Art, Architecture and Technology) for its modern architectural design. Enjoy lunch at a nearby café with views of the Tagus River.
- **Evening:** Return to the city center and have dinner at a trendy restaurant in the Chiado district, where you can enjoy contemporary Portuguese cuisine.

### Day 3: Bairro Alto and Cultural Exploration
- **Morning:** Start your day in the Bairro Alto district, known for its vibrant streets. Visit the São Roque Church, famous for its baroque architecture. 
- **Afternoon:** Explore the LX Factory, a creative hub with shops and eateries. Have lunch at one of the local food stalls or restaurants. 
- **Evening:** Enjoy a farewell dinner at a rooftop restaurant with views of the city, perhaps trying some local wine. 

## Practical travel tips
- Lisbon has a reliable public transport system, including trams and buses; consider getting a Lisboa Card for unlimited travel and discounts on attractions.
- The weather in Lisbon can be warm; dress in layers and wear comfortable shoes for walking.
- Portuguese cuisine often includes seafood; if you have dietary restrictions, check menus in advance.
- Be mindful of pickpockets in crowded areas, especially in tourist hotspots.
