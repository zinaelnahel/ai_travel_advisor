# Charming Lisbon: Architecture and Culinary Delights in 2 Days

## Trip summary
This 2-day trip to Lisbon, Portugal, focuses on exploring the city's rich architectural heritage and savoring authentic local food. With a moderate budget, you'll experience iconic landmarks, charming neighborhoods, and traditional Portuguese cuisine.

## Assumptions
- Arrival and departure times are flexible within the 2-day window.
- Moderate budget implies mid-range dining and accommodation.
- Departure city and flight costs are not included.
- Mobility is assumed to be comfortable for walking and public transport.

## Day-by-day itinerary

### Day 1: Historic Lisbon and Alfama District
- **Morning:** Start at Praça do Comércio, admire the grand architecture, then walk to the nearby Rua Augusta Arch for panoramic views. Explore the Baixa district's Pombaline architecture.
- **Afternoon:** Head to Alfama, Lisbon's oldest neighborhood. Visit the Lisbon Cathedral (Sé) and the National Pantheon. Enjoy lunch at a traditional tasca (small restaurant) serving dishes like bacalhau (salted cod).
- **Evening:** Experience a Fado performance in Alfama while dining on local specialties such as grilled sardines or caldo verde (kale soup).

### Day 2: Belém and Modern Lisbon
- **Morning:** Visit the Belém district to see the Jerónimos Monastery and Belém Tower, masterpieces of Manueline architecture. Try the famous Pastéis de Belém custard tarts at the original bakery.
- **Afternoon:** Explore the MAAT (Museum of Art, Architecture, and Technology) for contemporary architecture. Have lunch at a nearby café offering Portuguese petiscos (small plates).
- **Evening:** Return to central Lisbon to stroll through Chiado and Bairro Alto neighborhoods, enjoying a relaxed dinner at a local restaurant with a focus on fresh seafood and Portuguese wines.

## Practical travel tips
- Use Lisbon's efficient tram and metro system to get around; tram 28 covers many historic areas.
- Book Fado shows in advance, especially for dinner performances.
- Many historic sites have entrance fees; consider a Lisboa Card for discounts.
- Moderate weather is typical; comfortable walking shoes are recommended.
